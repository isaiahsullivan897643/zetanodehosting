To configure your server instances and process billing records in United States Dollars ($ USD), we collect:

  • Contact Details: First and last name, business name, physical billing address, and direct email address.

Your data is used strictly to deploy LiteSpeed NVMe server containers, route DNS records, auto-install Let’s Encrypt SSL certificates, answer incoming technical support inquiries, and prevent fraud or illegal automated scripting. Customer control layers and server metrics are protected with modern cryptographic hashing (TLS 1.3 / AES-256). Inactive server logs and automated backup snapshots are retained according to our compliance schedules, after which they are irreversibly scrubbed from our block storage clusters.
